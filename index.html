    How to Play

    • 10 questions, dealt at random from the Jain philosophy question bank, as you climb the Punya Ladder from 10 to 10,000 Punya.
    • There are 2 Padav (Milestones):
    • 🪜 Padav 1 — 5th Question: you must reach Question 5. This is mandatory to secure your minimum Punya.
    • 🪜 Padav 2 — 10th Question: reach the final question and answer all 10 correctly to become the Punya Vijeta.
    • After Question 5, you may choose to quit at any question and take home the Punya earned at the Padav you have reached.
    • Each question has 4 options. Tap one option to lock in your answer.
    • ⏱️ Every question has a 30-second timer, with the deck's own ticking sting.
    • 50:50 removes two incorrect options and restarts the timer — usable once per game.
    • +30 sec (top left) adds 30 seconds to the clock — available on every question, once per question.
    • Audience Poll opens a live poll for the host to use with the audience — usable once per game.
    • ❌ One wrong answer or the timer reaching zero ends the game — though from Padav 1 onward you keep the Punya you've secured.
    • 🏆 Answer all 10 questions correctly to become the Punya Vijeta!
